BETWEEN US — YOUTHFUL EXUBERANCE
Nine monochrome studies of gesture, proximity, and shared space.

EDITORIAL STATEMENT

Between Us is a nine-frame black-and-white photographic sequence built around two figures and the visual language of their interaction. The series moves through mirrored gestures, hand-made frames, shifts of distance, opposing directions, and moments of close attention.

Its subject is not youth as an appearance or an ideal. “Youthful Exuberance” names an emotional register: curiosity, spontaneity, play, and the freedom to communicate through gesture. The photographs make this visible through bodies and hands in relation, through faces that approach or turn away, and through the negative space that becomes active between the figures.

The monochrome treatment is essential to the series' coherence. It reduces the visual language to shape, tonal contrast, expression, and spatial rhythm. The open background gives the gestures room to read; the close framings make small shifts in position significant. The photographs should remain in their original black-and-white treatment, without colourisation or a homogenising preset.

PRESENTATION

Treat the sequence as a photographic essay, not a fast slideshow. Give the cover space to establish the visual grammar; vary image scale with intention; let close frames and more open compositions create a measured rhythm. Keep the interface restrained so the gestures remain the subject.

The series belongs within the wider Etherealism archive as a distinctly monochrome, human-centred photographic chapter. It should not be forced into the same treatment as the commercial, architectural, night, or skate collections.
