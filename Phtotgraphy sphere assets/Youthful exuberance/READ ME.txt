BETWEEN US — YOUTHFUL EXUBERANCE

Nine monochrome studies of gesture, proximity, and shared space.

This nine-frame black-and-white photographic sequence is built around two figures and the visual language of their interaction. It moves through mirrored gestures, hand-made frames, shifts of distance, opposing directions, and moments of close attention.

“Youthful Exuberance” names an emotional register—curiosity, spontaneity, play, and the freedom to communicate through gesture—not a physical ideal. The series derives its energy from the exchange between the figures and the negative space around them.
