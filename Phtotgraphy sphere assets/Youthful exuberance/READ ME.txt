YOUTHFUL EXUBERANCE — THE BRIGHTNESS OF BECOMING

This nine-frame series is about the feeling of being fully present: curiosity before certainty, spontaneity, movement, and the small discoveries that make ordinary time feel expansive. "Youthful" describes an emotional register, not a physical ideal. The images should feel lived-in and expressive rather than forced into a standardised idea of youth.
