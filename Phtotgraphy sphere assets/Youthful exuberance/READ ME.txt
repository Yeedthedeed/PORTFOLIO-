YOUTHFUL EXUBERANCE — BETWEEN US

A monochrome study of gesture, proximity, and shared space.

This nine-frame black-and-white photographic sequence is built around two figures and the visual language of their interaction. It explores gesture, expression, mirrored forms, changes of distance, and the negative space that separates or connects the figures.

The energy of the series comes from the exchange between bodies, hands, and faces—not from colour or spectacle. Hands become improvised frames; profiles create counterpoints; a turn of the head changes the composition. The word “youthful” describes an emotional register of spontaneity, curiosity, and expressive play, not a physical ideal.
