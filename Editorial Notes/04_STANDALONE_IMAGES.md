# Standalone Photographs
## Independent frames, not leftover assets

Four photographs sit directly in the photography root rather than inside a named album. They should be treated as deliberate independent works, not as files waiting to be sorted. The portfolio should make room for photographs that work as singular statements.

### Proposed presentation

**Section label:** *Single Frames*  
**Optional editorial label:** *Loose Frames — Independent Photographs*

| Canonical file | Proposed display title | Framer treatment |
|---|---|---|
| `Portals.png` | **Portals — A Threshold to Elsewhere** | An independent full-frame image with a restrained title. |
| `The Uprising.png` | **The Uprising** | Keep the original strong title; show as a standalone work. |
| `Untitled-2.jpg` | **Untitled Study 02** | Preserve the author's untitled status; use a discreet sequence label. |
| `Untitled-3.jpg` | **Untitled Study 03** | Preserve the author's untitled status; use a discreet sequence label. |

### Placement rules

- Give each image its own gallery item and detail view or lightbox.
- Do not force these works into Architecture, Nighthawks, or another album without evidence that they belong there.
- Use one image at a time in the editorial flow, with enough space to let the composition register.
- A standalone image may appear as a full-width interlude between album groups.
- Do not invent a location, subject, date, or backstory from the filename.
- Write accurate alt text only after each photograph has been inspected.

### Why this matters

A portfolio is not only a record of completed series. Some photographs are complete as single encounters. Keeping these four images visible preserves the breadth of the archive and gives the website a more personal editorial rhythm.
