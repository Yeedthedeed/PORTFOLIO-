## Ordering and accessibility rules

- Preserve the source-file name in the Framer CMS as the canonical asset identifier.
- Store display title and numeric order as separate fields.
- The frame labels above are intentionally non-descriptive. Replace them with image-specific editorial captions only after each still has been visually inspected.
- Write factual alt text from the actual frame, not from the movie title or poetic label.
- Keep credits, year, role, director, and production details tied to verified project information.
