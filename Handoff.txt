📋 The Actionable Bug List
1. Remove Placeholder Shapes (ScrollSphere.tsx)
Issue: Photographs are displaying background holders, borders, and box-shadows, making them look like framed cards rather than floating images.
Fix: Remove the border, box-shadow, and solid background color from the .face and <img> elements in ScrollSphere.tsx. The photographs should float cleanly.
2. Fix Filmography Layout Mismatch (FilmographyGallery.tsx)
Issue: The filmography landing page (FilmSphere.tsx) uses a spiral/ribbon layout, but the full gallery view (FilmographyGallery.tsx) uses a sphere layout. This breaks the visual metaphor.
Fix: Replace the buildSpherePositions logic in FilmographyGallery.tsx with the RIBBON_* (spiral) positioning logic found in FilmSphere.tsx.
3. Thin Out Film Frames
Issue: The film frames in the filmography sections are too heavy. They currently feature detailed sprocket holes and vertical number strips.
Fix: Simplify the frame design. Remove the sprocket holes and number strips. Replace them with a very thin, minimalist border (e.g., 1px solid rgba(180,160,120,0.15)) and a subtle shadow to retain the film-strip feel without the visual bulk.
4. Image Aspect Ratios & Orientation (ScrollSphere.tsx)
Issue: Horizontal and vertical photos are forced into predefined CARD_DEFS (fixed portrait, landscape, or square dimensions). This causes letterboxing and mismatched placeholders.
Fix: Remove the hardcoded fixed sizes. Let the images dictate their own card size using max-width and max-height constraints (e.g., max-width: 140px; max-height: 140px; object-fit: contain) so horizontal photos stay horizontal and vertical photos stay vertical.
5. Implement Full Album Curation Logic (ScrollSphere.tsx Viewer)
Issue: When a user clicks a photo, the viewer overlay only shows viewer.photo_id, viewer.caption, and viewer.album_title. It completely ignores the rich album data.
Fix: Update the viewer overlay to display the album's subtitle and intro from photographyManifest.ts. The viewer must display the full curatorial context (Album Title + Subtitle + Intro + Photo Caption).
✅ Definition of Done
The sphere floats without visible boxes.
The filmography gallery matches the spiral landing page.
Film frames are thin and elegant.
Horizontal and vertical photos display in their natural orientation without distortion.
Clicking a photo displays the full album curation text.
